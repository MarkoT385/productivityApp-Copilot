What parts of the development process with GitHub Copilot surprised you?
- The part tht suprised me the most was definetly how fastly and efficiently it can outline the whole website, and handle css styling as well as JavaScript, making a rough outline of an application in minutes.

What did you learn about the technology you used that you didn't know before?
- I learned that AI tools like Copilot perform the best when they are given short concentrted tasks, rather than broad undefined tasks, allowing the AI to execute your instructions in the most efficient way.

What would you do differently if you had to build this again?
- I would start with smaller pices rather than prompting it to create the whole outline of the app, I would focus on targeted portions first, like the calendar, sidebar, tasks... I think that that approach would produce less bugs, and create a better overall product.
